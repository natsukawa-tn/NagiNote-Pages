FAQ

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

If iCloud Sync is on in Settings (the default), your records appear automatically on a new iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID.

Can notifications show the medication name?

Set Settings > Notification Content to "Detailed" to include the medication name and dose.

What happens if I log a dose early?

That reminder is cancelled automatically and you will be reminded on the next scheduled day.

Can I export my data?

Settings > Export as CSV creates four CSV files (weight, doses, symptoms, expenses) in a ZIP. It is free.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Premium stays available until the end of the current period.

Premium isn't active on my new iPhone

Try Settings > Restore Purchases.

How do I change the language?

Settings > Language opens the iPhone Settings page where you can choose 日本語, English or 繁體中文 for this app.
