Features

🔔

Dose reminders

Works for weekly injections and daily oral medication. A follow-up reminder if a dose is missed, and one-tap logging right from the notification. Reminders for doses you already logged are cancelled automatically.

📈

Weight chart

One clean point per day, with your dose changes and goal weight on the same chart. Changes are never colored good or bad.

📝

Symptom notes

Log 11 symptoms such as nausea, constipation and headache as mild, moderate or severe, plus appetite and notes. See which symptoms came up over the last four weeks.

⏱

30-minute timer

After an oral dose, count down the 30 minutes until you can eat and drink — on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, without the medication name. (Premium)

🔄

Injection site rotation

Pick from six sites on a body diagram, with days since last use and a gentle suggestion for the least recently used site. (Premium)

💴

Expense tracking

Record consultation, medication, shipping and test costs and see monthly and total amounts. (Premium)

📄

Visit report

A one-page A4 PDF with medication and dose, dose records, weight and symptoms for the last 4 weeks, 3 months or a custom period. (Premium)

🔒

Private by design

No account. Face ID lock and discreet notebook or calendar app icons (Premium). The developer can never see your records.

🌍

3 languages

Japanese, English and Traditional Chinese.

Please note

This app is a logging tool, not a medical device. It does not give advice on diagnosis, treatment or dosing. Please consult the doctor or pharmacist who prescribed your medication.
